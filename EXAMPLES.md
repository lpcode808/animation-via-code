# Examples

The pieces these lessons came from, grouped by what drew them. This repo holds the lessons, not the pieces' source or videos; the pieces aren't linked from here. Pieces made from student work, or about other people, are left out. A dash means the piece confirmed lessons already listed rather than adding a new one.
